## Not bundled

### Lawn Siege art

The hidden arcade's **Lawn Siege** used to inline pixel art from
[JamesC01/ZombieGardenTD](https://github.com/JamesC01/ZombieGardenTD) under its
MIT licence. That art was **removed** and replaced with sprites drawn here — the
whole cast, plants, fusions, zombies and ground, is generated by
`tools/lawn_toon.py` from circles, polygons and arcs. Nothing is traced,
sampled, filtered or otherwise derived from anyone else's artwork; the fusion
plants are the base plants redrawn in another colour, not an image operation on
someone else's pixels.

The MIT notice for the art that was previously bundled remains in this file's
git history, which is where it belongs now that none of it ships.

**What this art is not.** It is not from Plants vs. Zombies. No PopCap asset is
used here, and none should be added — being free to download is not a licence to
redistribute. The game borrows the *genre's* conventions (a lane, a seed bar, a
wave meter) and, where they are named after the fan mod's own recipes, its
plant names. It borrows none of its pixels.
