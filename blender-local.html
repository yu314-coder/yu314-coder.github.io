Blender Local · iPhone and iPad

3D modeling and rendering in your hands

Blender Local is a 3D studio for iPhone and iPad built on Blender's own engine. Blender's bpy module is compiled into the app, so modeling, modifiers, Grease Pencil drawing, animation and rendering run Blender's real code on your device.

Renders with Cycles, EEVEE and Workbench Works offline, no account iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later Support below
Source on GitHub

Start from a template

File › New opens Blender's General, 2D Animation and Storyboarding scenes, or a ready-made template: a classroom, a studio showcase, a bouncing-ball animation, a low-poly landscape or a product turntable. A template opens looking through its camera, ready to render.

Model

Add meshes, curves, text, cameras and lights. Edit Mode has vertex, edge and face selection with Extrude, Inset, Bevel, Loop Cut, Knife Project, Spin, Bridge Edge Loops and more.

Move, Rotate and Scale with a touch gizmo or Blender's G, R and S keys, with snapping, proportional editing and the 3D cursor. Modifiers stack the way they do in Blender: Subdivision Surface, Mirror, Array, Bevel, Boolean, Solidify, Remesh, Geometry Nodes and more.

Draw and animate

Draw Mode is Grease Pencil drawing with a finger or Apple Pencil, with pressure, layers, onion skinning and keyframes. The Timeline edits keys, interpolation, easing and markers.

Render

Render stills and animations with Cycles on the Metal GPU, EEVEE or Workbench, and save animations as MP4 video. Materials expose every input of the surface shader.

Files and formats

Open and save .blend files, and import or export glTF, OBJ, USD, STL, PLY, FBX and Alembic. Your files live in the app's folder, which also appears in the Files app, and a finished render or video saves to Photos. UV editing, Image to 3D and a Python Scripting workspace round it out.

Private and offline

No account, no subscription and no ads. Your scenes and renders stay on your device. See the Blender Local privacy policy.

Support

Questions, a scene that won't open or render, or an idea: in the app, tap ? › Send Feedback, or email euler.yu@gmail.com and say which device and iOS or iPadOS version you use. Bugs can also be reported on GitHub.

Long Cycles renders use a lot of memory on iPhone; lower the samples or the resolution under Render, or use EEVEE, if a render stops.
